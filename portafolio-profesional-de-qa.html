How I work

A testing process that
prevents bugs, not just finds them.

01 — UNDERSTAND

Clarify before coding

Review requirements and acceptance criteria early. The cheapest defect is the one never written.

02 — DESIGN

Risk-based test design

Prioritize by user impact and business risk. Cover happy paths, edge cases, and accessibility from the start.

03 — AUTOMATE

Automate what repeats

Stable, maintainable suites with POM and BDD so regression runs in CI give a signal the team trusts.

04 — REPORT

Clear, actionable defects

Repro steps, evidence, and impact — so fixes are fast and releases are decisions, not guesses.
